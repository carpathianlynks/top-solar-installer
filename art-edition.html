The Challenge

Three compounding problems at scale

As the company grew across California and Texas, operational friction compounded. A fragmented dealer network, manual utility workflows, and scattered project data were creating gaps that would only widen.

01

Sales fragmented across dozens of dealer teams

Dealers relied on a third-party CRM. When licenses were revoked, there was no centralized platform — creating visibility gaps and no clean handoff from lead to job.

02

Utility applications 100% manual

Every interconnection application required Ops Managers to manually pull job data from multiple systems and work through each provider’s platform, one job at a time.

03

Project Managers lacked a way to surface risks early

Job data existed but was scattered. No intelligent way to surface urgent issues before they escalated.

What We Built

Three integrated modules

Sales Portal became the operational backbone. Automations eliminated manual utility workflows. AI Ops transformed how Project Managers engage with live job data.

01Sales Portal — Structured Operations for a Distributed Dealer Network⌃

Now used by 100+ dealer companies, 150+ teams, and 580+ users. Since early 2026 the sole source of truth for all lead and job management, replacing a decommissioned third-party CRM.

Full role hierarchy with scoped permissions. Two-step onboarding. Real-time dashboard with leads, pipeline trends, payments, and a rep leaderboard. Customer Verification sends homeowners an SMS to confirm their sales rep’s identity.

Since becoming sole source of truth: 5,000+ leads and 1,100+ jobs managed. Over 8,000 leads and 2,400+ jobs tracked across the full system lifetime.

Before this module, every interconnection application was 100% manual. It is now automated for three of four providers, each with a distinct flow. One click. The system does the rest.

400+ applications processed. SCE, PG&E, and SDG&E all automated. Each takes approximately 90 seconds.
