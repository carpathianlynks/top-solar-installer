The Challenge

Three compounding problems at scale

As the company grew across California and Texas, operational friction compounded. A fragmented dealer network, manual utility workflows, and scattered project data were creating gaps that would only widen at scale.

01

Sales was fragmented across dozens of teams

Dealers relied on a third-party CRM. When licenses were revoked, there was no centralized platform — creating visibility gaps, pipeline blind spots, and no clean handoff from lead to job.

02

Utility applications were burning hours, manually

Every interconnection application required Ops Managers to manually pull job data from multiple systems and step through each provider’s platform one job at a time.

03

Project Managers lacked a fast way to surface risks

PMs tracked dozens of active jobs with data scattered across CRM tables. No intelligent way to surface urgent issues or flag risks before they escalated.

What We Built

Three integrated modules

The Sales Portal became the operational backbone. Automations eliminated manual utility workflows. AI Ops transformed how Project Managers engage with live job data.

01Sales Portal — Structured Operations for a Distributed Dealer Network⌃

Now used by 100+ dealer companies, 150+ teams, and 580+ users. Since early 2026 it is the sole source of truth for all lead and job management, replacing a third-party CRM decommissioned across the network.

Full role hierarchy with scoped permissions. Two-step onboarding. Real-time dashboard with leads stats, trends, payments, and a leaderboard of top reps. Customer Verification step sends homeowners an SMS to confirm their sales rep’s identity.

Since becoming the sole source of truth: 5,000+ leads and 1,100+ jobs managed. Over 8,000 leads and 2,400+ jobs total across system lifetime.

Full role-based permission systemSMS-based onboarding & OTP authLead lifecycle managementReal-time pipeline dashboardCustomer Verification (fraud prevention)Payments data & rep leaderboard
02Automations — Eliminating Manual Utility Applications⌃

Before this module, every interconnection application was 100% manual. It is now automated for three of four providers in the network, each with a distinct underlying flow.

Ops Manager triggers one automation. System fetches job data, navigates the utility platform, completes the application. Missing data or errors surface immediately with exact details — PM fixes and restarts from that point.

400+ applications processed. SCE, PG&E, SDG&E all automated. Each submission takes approximately 90 seconds.

3 utility providers automated~90 sec per submissionReal-time status & audit logIntelligent error surfacing400+ applications processed100% manual previously
03AI Ops — Intelligence Layer for Project Management⌃

Continuously monitors active jobs for data changes and automatically runs analysis in the background. By the time a PM opens a job, the insights are already there — no manual trigger required.

Six configurable scenarios define what the AI looks for. Client team owns them fully — create, refine, and test against real data before deploying. Conversational chat lets PMs query any job naturally from live data.

700+ active jobs monitored. 400+ analyses per week. Zero manual triggers. All background.
