The Challenge

Three compounding problems at scale

As the company grew across California and Texas, operational friction compounded. A fragmented dealer network, manual utility workflows, and scattered project data were creating gaps that would only widen at scale.

01

Sales was fragmented across dozens of teams

Dealers relied on a third-party CRM to manage leads and users. When licenses were revoked, there was no centralized platform — creating visibility gaps, pipeline blind spots, and no clean handoff from lead to job.

02

Utility applications were burning hours, manually

Every interconnection application required Ops Managers to manually pull job data from multiple systems and step through each utility provider's platform one job at a time. At their volume, this was a significant and growing source of lost time.

03

Project Managers lacked a fast way to surface risks

PMs tracked dozens of active jobs simultaneously with data scattered across tables in their CRM. There was no intelligent way to surface which jobs needed immediate attention or flag risks early.

What We Built

A unified platform with three integrated modules

We designed and built a custom internal platform addressing each challenge directly. The Sales Portal became the operational backbone. Automations eliminated manual utility workflows. And AI Ops transformed how Project Managers work with live job data.

01Sales Portal — Structured Operations for a Distributed Dealer Network⌃

The Sales Portal is the operational backbone now used by over 100 dealer companies, 150+ teams, and 580+ users. Since early 2026, it has been the sole source of truth for all lead and job management — replacing a third-party CRM that was decommissioned across the network.

The platform supports a full role hierarchy with precisely scoped permissions at every level. Onboarding is a two-step flow: platform admins invite dealer companies; company owners then manage their own users, teams, and roles. A real-time dashboard surfaces lead stats, trends, payment data, and a leaderboard of top-performing reps. A Customer Verification step sends homeowners an SMS to confirm the identity of their sales rep — a fraud-prevention layer built into the core flow.

Since becoming the sole source of truth: 5,000+ leads and 1,100+ jobs managed. Over 8,000 leads and 2,400+ jobs tracked across the full system lifetime.

02Automations — Eliminating Manual Utility Applications⌃

Before this module, every interconnection application was submitted manually — an Ops Manager would pull job data from multiple systems and work through each utility's platform step by step, for every single job. That process is now automated for three of the four providers in the network.

An Ops Manager triggers an automation from within the portal. The system fetches the relevant job data, navigates the external utility platform, and completes the application automatically. If data is missing or an error is encountered, the system surfaces exactly where and why. The PM resolves it and restarts — the automation picks up from that point. Real-time status tracking and a full audit log give the team complete visibility throughout.

400+ applications processed. Three utility providers automated — each with a distinct underlying flow. Each automated submission takes approximately 90 seconds.

03AI Ops — Intelligence Layer for Project Management⌃

AI Ops is an AI-powered analysis and chat suite built directly into the portal. It continuously monitors active jobs for data changes and automatically runs analysis in the background — so by the time a PM opens a job, the insights are already there.

Each analysis is driven by configurable scenarios — structured instructions that define what role the AI takes and what to look for. The client's team owns these scenarios fully: they can create, refine, and test them against real job data before deploying. Currently six scenarios are active and running. On top of automated analysis, a conversational chat layer lets PMs query any job naturally — risks, blockers, history, colleague queues — with the AI responding from live job data.

700+ active jobs monitored in real time. 400+ jobs analyzed automatically in a typical week — zero manual triggers required.

The Bigger Picture

Why this matters for service businesses

This client's situation is not unique. Any service company managing a high volume of complex, multi-stakeholder projects — where data lives in one system, work happens in another, and people need fast, accurate context to make good decisions — faces the same structural problem.

The insight behind this platform is that AI doesn't need to replace the judgment of experienced professionals. It needs to reduce the time they spend gathering context, so that judgment can be applied sooner and more consistently.
