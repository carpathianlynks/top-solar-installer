The Challenge

Three compounding problems at scale

As the company grew across California and Texas, operational friction compounded. A fragmented dealer network, manual utility workflows, and scattered project data were creating gaps that would only widen at scale.

01

Sales was fragmented across dozens of teams

Dealers relied on a third-party CRM to manage leads and users. When licenses were revoked, there was no centralized platform — creating visibility gaps, pipeline blind spots, and no clean handoff from lead to job.

02

Utility applications were burning hours, manually

Every interconnection application required Ops Managers to manually pull job data from multiple systems and step through each utility provider's platform — one job at a time. At their volume, this was a significant and growing source of lost time.

03

Project Managers lacked a fast way to surface risks

PMs tracked dozens of active jobs simultaneously with data scattered across tables in their CRM. There was no intelligent way to surface which jobs needed immediate attention or flag issues before they escalated.

What We Built

A unified platform with three integrated modules

We designed and built a custom internal platform addressing each challenge directly. The Sales Portal became the operational backbone. Automations eliminated manual utility workflows. And AI Ops transformed how Project Managers work with live job data.

01Sales Portal — Structured Operations for a Distributed Dealer Network⌃

The Sales Portal is the operational backbone now used by 100+ dealer companies, 150+ teams, and 580+ users. Since February 2026, it has been the sole source of truth for all lead and job management.

The platform supports a full role hierarchy with precisely scoped permissions. Onboarding is a two-step flow: platform admins invite dealer companies; company owners manage their own users, teams, and roles. A real-time dashboard surfaces lead stats, trends, payment data, and a leaderboard of top-performing reps. A Customer Verification step sends homeowners an SMS to confirm the identity of their sales representative.

Since becoming the sole source of truth: 5,000+ leads and 1,100+ jobs managed. Total across the system: 8,000+ leads and 2,400+ jobs.

Full role-based permission systemSMS-based onboarding & OTP auth Lead lifecycle managementReal-time pipeline dashboard Customer Verification (fraud prevention)Payments data & leaderboard
02Automations — Eliminating Manual Utility Applications⌃

Before this module, the process was 100% manual — every interconnection application required Ops Managers to retrieve job data and step through each utility's platform individually. It's now automated for 3 of 4 providers.

An Ops Manager triggers an automation. The system fetches the job data, navigates the utility platform, and completes the application. If anything's missing, it surfaces exactly what and why. 400+ applications processed. Each takes approximately 90 seconds.

3 utility providers automated~90 sec per submission Real-time status trackingFull audit log Intelligent error surfacing400+ applications processed
03AI Ops — Intelligence Layer for Project Management⌃

AI Ops monitors active jobs for changes and automatically runs analysis in the background. By the time a PM opens a job, the insights are already there. 6 configurable scenarios define what the AI looks for — owned and maintained by the client's team.

700+ active jobs monitored in real time (~690 In Progress, 50+ On Hold). 50+ jobs analyzed in the last 24 hours. 400+ in the last 7 days — all automated, zero manual triggers.
